Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-10.

Your project data stays on your device unless you sign in. Planline runs entirely in your web browser. Without an account, projects, tasks and norms are saved only in your browser's local storage and are not uploaded anywhere. Files you import are read by your browser and are not uploaded.

Optional account

You can choose to sign in, with a link sent to your email address or with your Google account, so your projects are saved to your account and open on any device. If you do:

  • We store your email address and a user ID to identify your account, and copies of the projects you save (names, tasks, dates, quantities, progress and settings). Example projects and your edited norms library stay in your browser.
  • Sign-in and storage are provided by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Google processes this data on our behalf; see the Firebase privacy information.
  • Security rules allow only you, when signed in, to read or change your saved projects. We do not sell your data or use your project contents for advertising.
  • We use your email address only to sign you in. We do not send newsletters.
  • Delete your account and every saved project at any time from the account window in the scheduler (Sign out or delete account). Signing out keeps a copy in your browser unless you choose to remove it.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on those visits.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time.
  • See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Advertising never has access to the contents of your projects.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies or collect personal data, and it never sees your project data.

Fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers.

Hosting

The site is hosted on a static web host, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operations.

Local storage

The scheduler uses your browser's local storage to keep your projects, settings and theme choice. This stays on your device and is not used for tracking.

Children

Planline is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the contact page.
